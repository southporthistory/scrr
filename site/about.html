Susie Carson Research Room

The Research Room is part of the Southport Historical Society and preserves photographs, manuscripts, publications, family files, reports, recorded voices, and other materials relating to Southport and Brunswick County.

Research guidance

Using the catalog

  • The catalog describes; Google Drive delivers. Search and browse here, then open the preserved original in Drive.
  • Search follows catalog descriptions. It reaches titles, descriptions, names, places, subjects, and dates. It does not promise full-text search inside every scan.
  • Cataloging is ongoing. Names, places, subject terms, dates, covers, and descriptions improve as Society volunteers review the collection.

Permission to publish must be obtained in writing from the Director of the Susie Carson Research Room. The Society asks in exchange that it receive a copy of the finished work.
