Two clocks

When it happened, and when it was told

Much of this archive is Southport looking back at itself. Susie Carson's columns and the State Port Pilot's photo features ran in the 1970s, 80s and 90s but depict the 1890s, 1920s and 1940s. Both dates are recorded here, because they answer different questions.
